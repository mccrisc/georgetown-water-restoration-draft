About San Gabriel Water Restoration

A Georgetown-focused water damage team built around fast mitigation, clear communication, and credentials you can verify — not invented reviews or fake license numbers on a webpage.

Local positioning

San Gabriel Water Restoration serves homeowners and small businesses who need water damage restoration in Georgetown and nearby Williamson County. We emphasize emergency response, extraction, and drying — the work that stops a manageable leak from becoming a larger rebuild.

We speak as the local crew you call at night. We do not publish fabricated bios, stock “before and after” photos of jobs we did not perform, or star ratings we did not earn.

Our process

  1. Intake We learn the source, how long water has been present, and any safety concerns. You get a plain-language plan for the first visit.
  2. Mitigate Extract water, protect unaffected rooms, and set drying equipment where moisture readings justify it.
  3. Document Photos and moisture notes help you talk with your insurer. We help with documentation — we do not guarantee claim approval.
  4. Hand-off When drying goals are met, we walk you through next steps (repairs, pack-back, or further remediation if needed).
