Frequently asked questions

Straight answers for Georgetown water emergencies — no volume claims, no invented ratings.

How fast should I call after a leak or flood?

As soon as people are safe and the source is controlled (or you need help controlling it). Many secondary issues — including mold growth risk — rise after roughly 24–48 hours in warm, damp conditions. That is a general mitigation guideline, not a medical or insurance rule.

Do you work with homeowners insurance?

We help with documentation for mitigation work (photos, moisture notes, scope of what was done). Whether a claim is approved is up to your insurer and policy — we never promise claim approval.

What areas do you serve near Georgetown?

Georgetown is our home base. We also take calls in parts of Williamson County including Sun City, Jarrell, Weir, and the Round Rock edge when capacity allows. Details on the service areas page.

Is standing water always a DIY job?

Small, clean spills can often be handled carefully at home. Larger events, sewage involvement, ceiling saturation, or water near electrical systems need professional equipment and safety judgment. If unsure, call us — or call 911 if there is immediate danger.
