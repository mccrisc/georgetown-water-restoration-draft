Emergency & 24 Hour Water Damage in Georgetown

Active flooding, a burst supply line, or an overflowing appliance at 2 a.m. — we take emergency water damage calls around the clock in Georgetown, TX.

Life-safety first. If anyone is injured, if you smell gas, or if water is near live electrical panels you cannot safely shut off, call 911 or your utility. The steps below are general mitigation advice — not a replacement for emergency services.

What to do in the first hour

  1. Protect people Move family and pets to dry ground. Avoid wading near outlets, extension cords, or appliances still plugged in.
  2. Stop the source (if safe) Shut the main water valve or the fixture shutoff. Kill power to the affected circuit at the breaker only if you can reach it dry-footed.
  3. Call for emergency mitigation Dial (512) 555-0147 for 24 hour water damage help in Georgetown. Describe the source, how long water has been present, and whether sewage may be involved.
  4. Document before cleanup Take wide and close photos of each room. Note the time water started. This helps later insurance documentation — it does not guarantee a claim outcome.
  5. Limit further spread If safe, move valuables up and off wet floors. Do not run HVAC blindly through saturated ducts without guidance.
